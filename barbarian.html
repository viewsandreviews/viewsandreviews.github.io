Film Review

Barbarian

Bippidi Boppidi Boop
Director Zach Cregger
Year 2022
Starring Georgina Campbell, Bill Skarsgård, Justin Long

Horror. Simple; crude; satisfying.
And with a boop, a 10 out of 10.

Horror. Simple; crude; satisfying.

If one were to guess my passion for horror stemmed from the same guttural passion for sushi, one would be right.

More likely, what horror has to give that is worryingly more needed now than ever is the one non-psychoactive prescription for our overstimulated mind to react to any sensation. But even in a world of 5-second clipped entertainment outlets, a desire for a rollercoaster of clever entertainment, intertwined with actual artistic accomplishment is much needed.

✦

Enter Barbarian. That yearly movie that tries and succeeds in sewing together three confetti-sized plotlines at once, finally reunited in plot and quality. Three movies, exchanging a handful of shared characters, till it all converges into the motherly arms of a blasting ending.

As with all of the greatest horrors, it starts with the simplest of settings. On one of the many common rainy horror American nights, job-hunting Tess tries unsuccessfully to access her double-booked, shady-looking Airbnb. A bit of redundancy, I guess. Shame on you Wendy and Peter. If there's one thing that rhymes with fun, that's urine.

✦

As soon as her temporary accommodation sharer is introduced, the movie starts with a thrilling and satisfying guessing game of whack-a-mole. And with just two characters, a dumpster of a house and some clever lighting, Barbarian has you all in on the action. Glued on that low-budget screen.

But behold! Behold as Barbarian proves to be a better geisha than most, as it keeps on satiating body and soul with more than cheap thrills. When it tries comedy, it succeeds. In thrills? Bien sûre. Erotica? For the more cultured, gilf hunters of you. Something is indeed inflating, ms. Pelosi. And it ain't just the world's economy.

✦

Most surprisingly, what Barbarian does that most fail at is a genuinely touching social commentary, to the resonating sound of a boop.

And with a boop I'll leave you to find the rest of the 10 out of 10 that is gifted on your eyes by the newcomer and hopefully main-stayer Zach Cregger, for more boops our noses require.
