About

A professional nerd. A cold film stock. A questionable amount of free time.

Welcome aboard. This is Dr. Nabin Chowdhury speaking — a professional nerd who always found refuge in the warmth of cold film stocks. I have decided to exhume my undying love for cinema by sharing with my small world a handful of (mostly) satirical, NSFW movie reviews.

The blog started as a way to stop inflicting unsolicited opinions on people at dinner parties and redirect them somewhere people could safely ignore them at their own pace. It hasn't entirely worked, but the dinner parties have improved.

You'll find reviews of films and television series here — mostly recent, occasionally classic, always personal. The writing is mine. The opinions are mine. The scores are mine, and if you disagree with them, that is also fine and you are also wrong.

How Scoring Works
Doom Scroll
Score
Inverted scale, 1–10. How likely you are to reach for your phone during the film. A 1 means you forgot you owned a phone. A 10 means you were shopping for flights before the opening credits ended. Lower is better.
Linger
Factor
1–10, higher is better. How long the film sits with you after you've watched it. A 10 means you're still thinking about it three weeks later in the shower. A 1 means you've already forgotten the plot while writing this sentence.
Gut Punch
Score
1–10, higher is better. The film's capacity to hit you somewhere real — emotional, intellectual, or visceral. Not all films are trying to gut-punch you; the score accounts for ambition. A thriller that succeeds completely on its own terms scores higher than an art film that half-heartedly aims for profundity and misses.

The blog has been running since 2019. Over 72 posts and counting. Originally on Wix, now here.
