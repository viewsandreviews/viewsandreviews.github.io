Film Review

John Wick: Chapter 4

Arithmetics Prevails
Director Chad Stahelski
Year 2023
Starring Keanu Reeves, Donnie Yen, Bill Skarsgård

More legendary ass kickers +
more weapons + more bullets + more deaths
= the ultimate Joe Rogan experience.

Arithmetics prevails! So I imagine a proud Euler scream, after a showing of the fourth and (unlikely) last installment of the Baba Yaga.

Undoubtedly a nr. 4 that's truly greater than 1, 2, and 3, as greatness is what is added over the nigh-perfect representation of stuntman-turned-director, Chad Stahelski's wet dream. Great in the most addictive of senses, which makes math the star of a simple action formula: more legendary ass kickers + more weapons + more bullets + more deaths = the ultimate Joe Rogan experience.

✦

But we're all here for our favorite of stars, the human husky that is Keanu Reeves. Un-aging and "undying" as we had left him from the get-go, Reeves comes back once again to fulfill his destiny as Gen Z's action star of choice. A star that is more action than ever before. He's here to kick ass and chew bullets, and he's never out of bullets, as nobody is in this world of assassins.

What he's instead often out of are lines of dialogue, amounting to a number almost equal to 47 Ronin's box office, or fans. But who needs lines when you got the best action choreography of the Western hemisphere, sprinkled with a side of the imported Eastern moves and spring rolls.

✦

Wick is evermore immersed in his world of elegant and etiquetted crime, while transported to the bombastic 2-D reality of Hotline Miami when the pacing needs it. Wick numero 4 fully embraces the silliness of the macho hero archetype it relies upon and that it has pushed to the limit.

But don't let all of this fool ya. Dear Wick never forgets of his inner creamy heart, but instead keeps it protected in those exquisitely tailored bat-suits, until his final mission is completed, and my sobbing face is swollen up.

✦

So, while you may forget how it all started, or why it all started, what you will never forget are the lines of corpses and one-liners, or the furry friends we made along the way.
